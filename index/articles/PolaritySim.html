Use the arrow to see where the electron density is drawn. For a covalent bond, the negative region is pulled toward the more electronegative atom. For an ionic bond, the arrow points to the anion where the negative charge settles.

This builder uses elements from the first three periods. Noble gases are included as a reminder that they usually do not form stable molecules in this type of simulation.
